About TBT

A tiler who actually cares about the small stuff.

Tiled by Tyler is a small, owner‑operated tiling business based in Brisbane, focused on quality over volume.

I’m Tyler, the tiler behind TBT. I’ve spent years on the tools across bathrooms, kitchens, floors and outdoor areas around Brisbane. Over that time I’ve learnt that the difference between “good enough” and “that looks unreal” is all in the prep and the details.

I keep job numbers low so I can stay on‑site, on the tools and in control of the finish. You’ll deal with me from quote through to final clean‑up—no disappearing act once the job starts.

Whether it’s a small splashback or a full bathroom, I’ll walk you through tile layout, grout colour, trims and falls so you know exactly what you’re getting before the first tile is laid.
