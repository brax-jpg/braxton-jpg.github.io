Services

Everything from prep to grout clean‑up.

TBT can handle full bathroom builds, small repairs and everything in between. The focus is always on straight lines, solid prep and tidy sites.

Bathrooms

Full bathroom tiling

Strip‑out, screeding, waterproofing and tiling for new bathrooms and renovations. Layouts planned around falls, niches and fixtures.

Ideal for: Renovations, new builds, ensuites
Kitchens

Splashbacks & feature walls

Clean splashbacks and feature walls behind cooktops, vanities and bars. Options for stacked, brick bond, herringbone and more.

Ideal for: Kitchens, laundries, bars
Floors

Internal & external floors

Floor tiling over concrete or prepared substrates. Careful attention to lippage, grout joints and transitions to other floor finishes.

Ideal for: Living areas, entries, patios
Wet areas

Waterproofing

Membrane installation to Australian standards for showers, bathrooms, balconies and other wet areas before tiling.

Ideal for: Bathrooms, laundries, balconies
Prep

Levelling & screeding

Floor levelling and screeds to create the right falls and flatness before tiles go down, reducing lippage and pooling.

Ideal for: Uneven slabs, shower bases
Repairs

Repairs & re‑grouting

Replace cracked tiles, fix drummy areas and refresh tired grout to extend the life of your existing tiling.

Ideal for: Localised issues, cosmetic refresh
